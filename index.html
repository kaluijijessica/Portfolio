01 / About

Data, technology and people

I work across data science, data engineering, business intelligence and software development. At First Quantum Minerals, I collaborate with managers, system owners and business teams across Safety, Mining, Process, Human Resources and Training.

My work often begins with an operational or reporting problem. I help define the measures, investigate the available data, resolve quality issues and develop a practical solution. Depending on the problem, that solution may be an ETL pipeline, analytical dataset, Power BI report, automated alert or web application.

My software-development background helps me think beyond the final dashboard. I consider the database structure, application logic, user experience, automation and how the solution will be maintained after delivery.
